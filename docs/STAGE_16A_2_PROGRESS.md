## Durable ownership contract

Final product ownership remains Recording-owned.

During an active capture, before the final Recording row exists, an annotation may be durably staged as `PENDING_SESSION`. It must later promote to `ATTACHED_RECORDING`; this staging state is not a second long-term ownership model.

Only `recordingId` is an ownership foreign key. Transcript, revision, transcript segment, realtime lineage, and speaker values stored on annotations are snapshots/provenance and must not cascade-delete annotations.

## Timeline contract

Primary durable time identity is sample-based.

Supported anchor domains:

- `CANONICAL_RECORDING`
- `REALTIME_MEDIA_SEGMENT`

No Stage 16A.2 code may fabricate a continuous playback timeline across recorder-card disconnect gaps.
